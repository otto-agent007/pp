# Technician Workflow

1. Open job
2. Enter property (geofence)
3. Job auto-starts
4. Capture photos
5. Fill forms
6. Log chemicals
7. Capture signature
8. Complete job
9. Sync all data

## Offline Behavior
- Everything works offline
- Actions queued locally
- Sync retries automatically

## Customer Visibility
- Photos visible immediately after sync
- Reports downloadable
- Payments accessible via portal
